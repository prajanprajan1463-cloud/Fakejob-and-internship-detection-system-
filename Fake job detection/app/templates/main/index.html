Threat Awareness

Common Job Scam Warning Signs

Stay alert for these frequent tactics employed by fraudulent recruitment operations.

Upfront Payment Demands

Demands for registration fees, training deposits, or purchasing work equipment from a specified vendor are fraudulent.

Interviews Exclusively on Chat Apps

Legitimate companies conduct structured interviews via video or phone. Unofficial Telegram or WhatsApp channels are red flags.

Free Webmail Addresses

Recruiters claiming to represent enterprise tech firms while emailing from generic @gmail.com or @yahoo.com accounts are impostors.

Instant Job Offers Without Screening

Unsolicited job offer letters sent without any resume review, portfolio inspection, or technical evaluation are high-risk.
